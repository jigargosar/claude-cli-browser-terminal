Filler, so the pane can scroll. The cache holds the last 50 replies in memory. A refresh drops it, which matches Decision 2.2: the right panel may be blank after a refresh. Keep replies in a Map keyed by message id. Evict the oldest entry once the map passes 50. Write nothing to disk. The transcript already holds every reply.

    
Two risks remain. A very long reply may be slow to render, and a rewind leaves stale entries behind.

    
Open question: should a rewind clear the whole cache, or only the entries after the rewind point?
